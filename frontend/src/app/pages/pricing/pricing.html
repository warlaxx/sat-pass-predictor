That budget is for people looking at the sky, not for integrations: there is no anonymous batch endpoint.

How quotas count

When does a monthly quota reset?
At 00:00 UTC on the first day of each calendar month - not on your subscription anniversary. The first, partial month has the full quota.
Does a cached answer count?
Yes. A call is a call, whether it was propagated or served from the prediction cache.
How does a batch count?
One request per prediction (satellites × sites), failed entries included, against every limit. A batch that does not fit the remaining quota is refused whole and consumes nothing.
What happens at the limit?
A 429 with Retry-After, resetsAt and which limit was reached. There is no overage billing: nothing is ever charged beyond the plan.
Do upgrades or key rotation reset usage?
No. Usage belongs to the account's month; changing plan, rotating or revoking a key never resets it.
Is there an SLA?
Not yet. The service runs on an instance that can sleep, and an SLA would be a sentence rather than a commitment until it does not. See the live status.
